# Bubu Jobs 💌

Hi Bubu! This little app keeps an eye out for new Product Management jobs and sends them to your email, so you have one less thing to check.

It checks [Jobright’s Product Management new-grad job list](https://github.com/jobright-ai/2026-Product-Management-New-Grad) every 30 minutes. That’s the only place it pulls jobs from right now. It looks for roles like Associate Product Manager, Product Analyst, and Product Operations, and filters out obvious senior roles and unrelated jobs.

You’ll get an email when there are new matches, with the job details and a link to each listing. It remembers jobs it has already found, so you won’t keep getting the same list. If there’s nothing new, there’s no email.

## When you find a job you like

1. Create a [Jobright account](https://jobright.ai/) and sign in.
2. Open the job link in your email.
3. Click **“Manually Apply”** on Jobright to go to the company’s career site.
4. Review the requirements and submit your application there.

The app finds opportunities for you; you choose which ones to apply to. Keep Gmail notifications on if you’d like a heads-up when a new email arrives. 💛
